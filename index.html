Welcome to TembaCuts

Who We Are

Welcome to TembaCuts, Hammanskraal's premier grooming destination. We blend elite precision with authentic, fresh Kasi flavor. Our master barbers deliver top-notch cuts, clean fades, and premium grooming tailored for you.

Our Services

Precision Haircut

Crisp, sharp line-ups tailored to your head shape. Tailored cuts executed with razor accuracy for a pristine finish.

Fade & Taper

Flawless skin fades and low tapers with seamless blending. Smooth gradients that keep your haircut looking fresh for days.

Beard Sculpting

Clean razor edging, hot towel conditioning, and sharp beard shaping. Disciplined facial hair grooming for a refined look.

Sponge Twists

Defined, texturized curl patterns for short and medium hair. Neat, uniform twists sealed with quality products.

Dreadlocks Maintenance

Thorough scalp cleansing, re-twisting, and palm rolling. Keeps your locs tight, healthy, and impeccably structured.

Chiskop & Razor Wash

Ultra-smooth bald head shave paired with an antiseptic wash. Mirror-clean precision with absolute skin protection.
